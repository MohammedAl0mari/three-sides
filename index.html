Room

How to play
  • On your turn, click a dot, then click one of the highlighted dots to draw a straight line between them. A line can cover 1, 2 or 3 segments.
  • A line may not run over a segment that is already drawn.
  • If your line closes the third side of one or more small triangles, you claim them all.
  • Play then passes to the next player, whether or not you closed a triangle.
  • The game ends when every segment is drawn. The player with the most triangles wins.
  • Online: one player creates a room and shares the code or invite link. Up to three friends join from their own devices, then the host starts the game. Each device controls one colour.
